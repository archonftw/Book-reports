                                        Identifying Architectural Characterstics




Architects and domain stakeholders speak different language

Translation for both
Mergers and acquisitions                                Interoperability, scalability, adaptability, extensibility
Time to market                                          Agility, testability, deployability
User satisfaction                                       Performance, availability, fault tolerance, testability, deployability, agility, security
Competitive advantage                                   Agility, testability, deployability, scalability, availability, fault tolerance
Time and budget                                         Simplicity, feasibility


Explicit Characterstics: They appear in a requirements specification as part of necessary design.
Implicit Characterstics: They do not appear in requirements list. Ex: Availability,Reliability,Security

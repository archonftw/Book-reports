









Applications can support a huge number of architecture characterstics... but shouldn't, as support for each characterstic, adds complexity to the design

Architecture characterstics:
    1. Implicit :Rarely appear in requirements,yet theyre necessary for project's success, availability,reliability,security
    2. Explicit :They appear in requirements documents or other specific instructions

Architecture characterstics exist along a broad spectrum of software system,modularity to sophisticated operational concerns, such as scalability and elasticity

Operational characterstics: Availability, Continuity, Performance, Recoverability, Reliability, Robustness, Scalibility

Structural characterstics: Configurability, Extensibility, Installability, Leverageability, Localization, Maintainability, Portability, Supportability, Upgradeability

Cross cutting characterstics: Accessibility,Archivability,Authentication,Authorization,Legal,Privacy,Security,Supportability,Usability

Architects rarely encounters a situation where they are able to design a system and maximize every singel architecture characterstic.
NEVER SHOOT FOR BEST ARCHITECTURE,BUT RATHER THE LEAST WORST ARCHITECTURE
